### Early school start for the suburbs appears to bring record-breaking first fair days

As I expected, the early school start dates (1-2 weeks before Labor Day) had a big impact on daily fair attendance, but not the exact impact I forecasted. The early school start for suburban districts appears to have resulted in a much higher than average attendance on the first Thursday and Friday, also setting all-time daily attendance records. My mistake was assuming that it would cause higher attendance than the last several years because fewer people would be traveling for the holiday if they had to be in town for school. Now I know, it means the attendance will be front loaded before school starts in the suburbs. For total attendance, I forecasted a 4% increase over 2025, but we landed under by about 1%. My furthest forecast from the actual was the second Friday (Day 9) at about 26.5% over, and my closest forecast was on the first Monday (Day 5) at about 2.4% over.
